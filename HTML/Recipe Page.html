Pizza Recipe

To make a pizza you needs a base made of flour, cheese, mayonise, ketchup and different toppings to be baked in a oven

Ingredients

  • flour
  • cheese
  • ketchup
  • tomato

Instructions

  1. First you have to make the base with the flour.
  2. Then you have to apply sauce on base.
  3. After that you have to apply liquid cheese and sprinkle your favourite toppings on it.
  4. Then you bake the whole pizza in oven for 30 minutes.
